problem: 政务办事文件读不懂、趟跑不对——带什么材料、走哪几步、去哪办，全靠人猜。
whatIDid: 做一个对话式导办小程序，按 MVP 规格设计 20 项能力：办事卡片（材料 / 步骤 / 渠道 / 时限 / 费用 / 常见坑 / 官方源）、步骤卡分享、长辈版大字与语音朗读、"孩子发给我的"转介入口、一键拨 12345、纠错工单闭环与双人审核版本管理、模型调用成本熔断。
challenge: 只导办、不代办——每句回答都要落回官方文件的出处。成本上立了比例：九成常见问题走结构化命中，只允许一成兜底走大模型。目前完成的是地基阶段，其余按阶段推进中。
titleEn: "Public-Services RAG Guide Assistant (Mini Program)"
problemEn: "Government procedure documents are hard to read and trips go wrong — what to bring, which steps, where to go, all guessed by hand."
whatIDidEn: "A conversational guide mini program specced as a 20-item MVP: service cards (materials / steps / channels / deadlines / fees / common pitfalls / official source), shareable step cards, a senior mode with large type and read-aloud, a 'sent by my kid' entry, one-tap to the 12345 hotline, a correction-ticket loop with two-person review and versioning, and a cost breaker on model calls."
challengeEn: "Guiding only, never acting for you — every answer has to land back on a source in the official files. Cost carries a rule of thumb too: nine in ten common questions are answered from structured data, and only the remaining one in ten may fall back to the model. What exists today is the foundation stage; the rest is being built phase by phase."
